Today

Since January 2026 I've worked as a psychologist, both in my own practice and at Clínica Primoris. I see children, adolescents and adults, some in person and some online. They come to therapy for very different reasons: anxiety, difficulty dealing with emotions, low self-esteem, relationships, body image and eating. I work with cognitive-behavioural and contextual approaches and often bring in compassion-focused and mindfulness-based practices, the same ideas that run through my research.

For confidentiality, no patients, screens with patient information or clinical records appear in these photos.

Where I learned

I saw my first patients at the university's Psychology Training Clinic, over two supervised practicums between 2024 and 2025. The first was in cognitive-behavioural therapy and brief psychotherapy. The second focused on contextual therapies, Compassion-Focused Therapy and eating disorder care, which we provided together with the Nutrition team through joint supervision and case discussions. I followed each case from the first assessment through case formulation, treatment planning and follow-up.

Children and families

In 2023 I joined the Pediatric Outpatient Follow-up Program at the university hospital. Under supervision, I assessed children from more than twenty families through anamnesis and clinical observation, supported their parents and caregivers, and discussed each case with the hospital's interdisciplinary team. Earlier, in 2022, I had spent a semester as a school psychology intern at a public school in Serra, assessing its teaching practices and working with teachers and students on school integration.

Aug 2024 – Oct 2025

Positive Parenting Promotion Program

University outreach programme, UFES · Undergraduate team member and teaching assistant

FamiliesWorkshopsProfessional training

This outreach programme, coordinated by Professor Fabiana Pinheiro Ramos, works with parents, caregivers and the professionals around them, focusing on caregiver–child relationships and positive discipline. With the team, I organised and led workshops and talks in health units, hospitals, a juvenile court and online, including a talk for about fifty municipal education professionals. At the university hospital, I co-led sessions with around seven families under weekly faculty supervision.

The programme also ran a professional training course in positive parenting, with about fifty participants, most of them practitioners. As a teaching assistant, I mentored a group of participants, led planning meetings and gave individual feedback on their assignments. The course later became a book that I co-edited, and the programme received an Honourable Mention at UFES's Maria Filina Award for Extension Merit in 2026.

Oct 2024 – Oct 2025

Mindfulness for emotional regulation

Workshop facilitator · UFES
Mindfulness for Emotional Regulation and Well-Being Among University Students

StudentsWorkshops

A series of psychoeducational workshops for university students at UFES. This was a separate project from the hospital groups: it was aimed at students, not patients, and focused on introducing mindfulness practices and ways of noticing and regulating emotions as part of everyday student life, with wellbeing in mind.

I facilitated workshops for about twenty students and took care of the practical side: organising the sessions, administering questionnaires and reporting the results.

Mar 2023 – Sep 2024

Teaching and science communication

Teaching assistant · Instituto Fratelli

At Instituto Fratelli, an institute that offers continuing education for psychologists, I assisted instructors in online courses, helped onboard and train new team members, wrote scientific texts and reports, and created psychology content for the institute's social media page, which has about 80,000 followers.

2023 – 2024

A student neuroscience league

Academic League of Neuroscience and UFES Neuroscience Lecture Series

The Academic League of Neuroscience brings together UFES students interested in the brain and behaviour. As its research director, from August 2023 to July 2024, I helped members choose research topics, organise their schedules and take part in scientific discussions. I also worked to keep the group connected and engaged in the league's activities.

In the second half of 2023 I also helped organise the UFES Neuroscience Lecture Series, a national series of academic talks. I worked on the programme, invited speakers and handled communication with participants and the technical side. We later presented the series at the VI Luso-Brazilian Seminar on Science Communication.

Jan 2026 – Present

BRASA

Graduate mentee · Brazilian Student Association

MentoringGraduate admissionsCommunity

I was selected as one of 30 graduate mentees in BRASA's mentoring programme for Brazilian students pursuing education abroad. More than 8,000 people applied for 60 places (30 graduate and 30 undergraduate), an acceptance rate of about 1%. I joined while preparing my applications to graduate programmes abroad.

  • Weekly activities with experienced mentors who study at universities abroad.
  • Guidance throughout the international graduate admissions process.
  • A global network of Brazilian students and professionals.
  • Support for Brazilians pursuing international academic careers.
